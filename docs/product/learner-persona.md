# Learner Persona & Product Promise

Source: Code2Prod Founding Product Blueprint v0.1 (Sections 3, 23, 24).

## Who this is for

**Name:** Rafi, the CSE trainee (representative persona, not a real person)

- 18-27 years old, Bangladeshi CSE/CS student, recent graduate, or serious self-taught learner.
- Knows basic programming (some Python or Java from university, maybe a couple of tutorials).
- Has a basic laptop and internet; mobile is a secondary device, not primary.
- Comfortable reading technical English but thinks and reasons more easily in Bangla.
- Has followed YouTube tutorials and maybe one bootcamp-style course, but has never worked a ticket,
  opened a real pull request against someone else's codebase, or been on the other end of a production
  incident.
- Wants: a job-ready portfolio, clarity about what "DevOps," "Platform," and "SRE" actually mean day to
  day, and proof (not just a certificate) that they can do the work.
- Cannot justify spending BDT 10,000+ on a bootcamp with uncertain payoff. Will pay a small amount
  (under ~BDT 1,000 per stage) if the value is obvious before checkout.

## The gap we are closing

Rafi does not lack tutorials. Rafi lacks **context**: how a change actually travels from a laptop to
production, how a team coordinates that, and what happens when it breaks. Code2Prod closes this gap by
making every lesson a piece of real-shaped work (a ticket, a broken build, an incident) inside one
continuously evolving fictional company, rather than a video followed by a quiz.

## Product promise

> Your first engineering job should not be your first experience doing real engineering work.

Code2Prod puts learners inside a simulated software company (**ByteBangla Technologies**) where they
receive tickets, work on repositories, ship software, investigate incidents, and leave with verifiable
evidence of skill — commits, pull requests, tests, incident write-ups — not just a certificate.

**Brand promise:** We do not sell hours of content. We give learners engineering work.

**Positioning:** Code2Prod — your first engineering experience before your first engineering job. This
is deliberately not a promise of employment, salary, or guaranteed placement. The product gives
realistic practice and evidence; the learner still has to earn the job.

## What Rafi should be able to say after finishing a course

> "I did not just watch someone use these tools. I received requirements, changed a real repository,
> shipped software, watched it fail, investigated evidence, fixed the problem, and can show what I did."

## Non-goals (avoid overclaiming)

- No guaranteed jobs, no salary promises, no "internationally recognized certificate" claims unless true.
- No "best course in Bangladesh" / "cheapest course" framing — price is about accessibility, not the
  headline.
- No "become an expert in 30 days" — the promise is realistic practice, not mastery speed.
